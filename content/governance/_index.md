Post Fiat proposes that what comes after fiat is a new social model, one where deterministic judgment plays a central role in governance in place of the opaque committees that run the XRPL. Each entry below records a question put to a pinned model, the answer, and the receipts that let anyone check that independent machines produced the same bytes.
