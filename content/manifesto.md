## Machine intelligence will compound capital on chain.

Models already read filings, price risk and write the code that moves money faster than the institutions that employ them. The next step is plain: they will hold capital, rebalance it and settle it directly. Wherever that happens needs settlement that is fast, private, verifiable and neutral. Fiat rails were built for clerks. This is for what comes after.

## Post Fiat is the chain where that will happen.

It takes the best of XRP (known validators, deterministic finality, a fixed native supply, fee burn, no validator subsidy) and evolves it for on-chain capital markets. It is private, quantum-resistant from genesis, and governed on chain through Cobalt. It is XRP 2.0: the private, quantum-resistant internet of value for the superintelligent era.

## Value must be proven, not asserted.

A portfolio on Post Fiat is a NAVCoin: a floating-value unit whose reserves, supply, methodology and custody can each be inspected on their own. The ledger creates and retires units only against a finalized, proven net asset value. If the reserves fall, the NAV falls. Nothing is pegged; nothing is typed in.

## Exposure must move in private.

A large order should not have to announce itself to a public pool. Asset-Orchard settles both legs of an exchange in one atomic, shielded transition, and enforces per-asset conservation inside the proof. Privately swap any asset on chain, at low cost. Access market-neutral tokenized portfolios without broadcasting your book.

## The money underneath must be hard.

Native supply is fixed at genesis. Every fee burns. No validator is paid by inflation, and no protocol treasury can mint. A settlement layer for risk assets should rest on an asset that cannot be diluted to fund its own operators.

## Intelligence is a network, not a firm.

Task Node is the community intelligence layer: pseudonymous contributors and their agents take work, publish evidence, are verified and are paid. Instead of a crowd that can only post, hold and hope, the network internalizes its own edge instead of donating it to outside firms.

## The process is reflexive.

Contributors build the network that rewards contributors. Each verified piece of work makes the next more valuable, and each product built on the chain gives the community more to do. The fiction of a machine-native capital market is made real by the people who act as if it already exists.

## Old rules judge new rules.

Trust in validators is protocol state, not a file someone publishes. Every change to who validates is a typed object, checked by the current registry before it takes effect. Models may read the evidence; they never hold the pen.
