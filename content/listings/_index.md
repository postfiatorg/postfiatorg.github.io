Post Fiat L1 (PFTL) is a Rust settlement chain in the operating shape of the XRP Ledger: known validators, deterministic finality, a fixed-supply native asset (PFT) and burned fees. It adds three things XRP does not have: ML-DSA-65 post-quantum signatures for accounts and validators, an optional Orchard/Halo2 shielded pool, and NAVCoins, assets issued only against proven reserves. The [whitepaper](/whitepaper/) describes the design and maps each claim to its proof and source.
