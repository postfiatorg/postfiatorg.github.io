How it works

Bring assets from any chain. Value them cheaply. Swap them privately.

Post Fiat is a chain for verified cross-chain assets. Stocks, crypto and whole trading strategies come in as tokens whose backing the network checks, so they can be priced without auditors and traded without showing your hand.

Turn any portfolio into one verified token.

A portfolio token, which we call a NAVCoin, wraps a basket of assets held anywhere (Ethereum, Hyperliquid, a broker) into one token. Validators check the reserves behind it before every price update, so anyone can buy, sell or lend it at its real value without paying a fund administrator, an auditor or a trust premium.

Economics of the NAVCoin →

Swap size without moving the market.

On a public exchange a large order announces itself: you pay for slippage and get front-run. On Post Fiat both sides of a trade settle together in one private transaction at the token's checked value, and nobody sees the order until it is done.

How private swaps settle →

Hold assets that stay safe for decades.

Every account and validator signs with keys quantum computers cannot break, from day one, so what you hold on Post Fiat will not need an emergency move when they arrive.

How the bridge stays post-quantum →

Know exactly who runs the network.

The validator list lives on chain. Adding or removing a validator takes a vote of the existing set, recorded for anyone to check, so no single company can quietly change who settles your trades.

How Cobalt governs validators →

Settle on money that cannot be printed.

PFT has a fixed supply and every fee is burned, so using the network makes the base asset scarcer over time.
